WALEGO · PRODUCT 2 · MATERIALS

Selfie video question set

12 questions, ColdIQ-framework structure. The brief Johann sends directly to each nominated client. Client films one 1–2 min video answering all 12 in order, ~5–15 seconds per answer.

Quick filming tips for the client:
  • Film horizontal (landscape) on your phone
  • Face a window or soft light — never have a window behind you
  • Earbuds with a mic = great sound
  • Pause briefly between answers so we can cut cleanly
  • Have your real numbers ready (Q1 + Q8 need them)

The 12 questions

INTRO · HERO METRIC · ~10s
Q1Tell us your name, your company, and the one number that proves Walego worked for you.

Example: "I'm X, founder of Y. With Walego we booked 47 meetings in 30 days." This becomes the hero line on the page.

FOUNDATION · ABOUT YOU · ~10s
Q2What does your company do, and who do you sell to?

Your product + your ICP in plain English.

BEFORE WALEGO · THE STATE · ~10s
Q3Before Walego, how were you handling LinkedIn outreach and prospecting?

Manual? Multiple tools? VAs? In-house SDRs?

BEFORE WALEGO · THE PAIN · ~15s
Q4What were the 2 or 3 things that were really broken about how you were doing it?

Time wasted, low reply rates, bad data, expensive tools, no visibility, can't scale, etc.

DECISION · WHY WALEGO · ~10s
Q5Why did you choose Walego over the other options you'd looked at?

What stood out — feature, price, recommendation, demo experience.

PROCESS · WHAT YOU BUILT · ~15s
Q6Walk us through what you actually do with Walego today.

ICP / list building, campaign sequences, follow-ups, CRM, AI agents, integrations.

PROCESS · THE "AHA" MOMENT · ~10s
Q7What was the moment you realized "okay — this actually works"?

One specific feature, result, or week where it clicked.

OUTCOME · THE NUMBERS · ~15s
Q8Give us the real results in numbers: meetings, replies, deals, revenue, hours saved.

Bring as many specific metrics as you can. Compare to before if possible ("went from X to Y").

OUTCOME · WORKING WITH WALEGO · ~10s
Q9How would you describe what it feels like to use Walego day-to-day?

Product, team, support, speed — what's the experience like vs your last tool?

LESSON · WHAT YOU'D TELL OTHERS · ~10s
Q10What's the biggest thing you'd tell another founder, agency, or sales team about outbound after using Walego?

One principle or hard-won lesson.

RECOMMENDATION · WHO IT'S FOR · ~10s
Q11Who should absolutely use Walego, and who shouldn't bother?

Be specific about the fit. Disqualifying makes recommendations 10× stronger.

HERO LINE · CLOSER · ~5s
Q12Finish this line on camera: "If you're serious about LinkedIn outreach, Walego is…"

One short, punchy sentence. This is the closing quote we'll use on the website hero.
