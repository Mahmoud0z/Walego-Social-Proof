WALEGO · PRODUCT 3 · MATERIALS

Premium GTM Session — Conversation Script

Three parts, 15–30 minutes total. Written by Marketing, run by Johann on Google Meet (Riverside for recording). The structure is fixed; the questions are the script.

Reminders for Johann before recording:
  • Be conversational, not interview-y. The script is a safety net, not a teleprompter.
  • Pause 1 second between answers so Marketing can cut clean clips.
  • Watch the time. Each part is roughly 5–10 minutes. Don't stack Part 1.
  • Part 3 is non-negotiable. It's why this product exists. Reserve at least 5 min for it.
PART 1 ~5–8 min

Client story

Set the stage. The guest's business, market, growth challenges before Walego.

Johann opens (~30 sec)
"Welcome [first name], thanks for doing this. We're going to do something a little different today — not a testimonial, more of a strategic conversation in three parts. First we'll talk about your business and the world you operate in. Then how we ended up working together and what that looked like. And to close, I'll share the GTM methodology behind what we did. Sound good?"
Johann's questions to the guest
Q1.Tell us who you are and what your company does.

Goal: 30-second intro. Company, role, ICP.

Q2.What does the market look like for what you sell — who is your buyer, and what's hard about reaching them?

Goal: situate them. Specific buyer, specific outreach pain.

Q3.Before we started working together, what did your outbound motion look like?

Goal: paint the "before" state. Tools, processes, team, results.

Q4.What was the #1 thing that was broken? What was costing you the most?

Goal: get the central pain on record. Time? Reply rates? Cost? Visibility?

Q5.What had you already tried that didn't work, and why?

Goal: pre-emptively disqualify the alternatives prospects will be evaluating.

PART 2 ~5–8 min

Walego partnership

The middle act. Why us, what we built together, what changed.

Johann's bridge
"So that's the world you were operating in. Now let's talk about how we ended up working together and what we actually built."
Johann's questions
Q6.How did you find Walego, and what made you decide to try it specifically?

Goal: the trigger event + the deciding factor. Useful for our acquisition copy.

Q7.Walk us through onboarding and the first 30 days. What did we set up together?

Goal: show that Walego is a system, not a tool. ICP, signal stack, sequences.

Q8.What was the first moment where you went "okay, this is working differently"?

Goal: the inflection point. Anchor for the case study headline.

Q9.Talk us through how the campaigns evolved. What did we change, drop, double down on?

Goal: show iteration is part of the methodology. We didn't get it right on day 1.

Q10.Give us the results in real numbers — meetings, pipeline, revenue, time saved.

Goal: the proof. Real numbers, compared to before. Hero metric of the whole session.

Q11.What changed inside your team because of this? Headcount, processes, role of outbound?

Goal: business transformation, not just metric improvement.

PART 3 ~5–10 min

Johann explains the method

The flagship moment. This is why the product exists. Johann turns the conversation outward and explains the GTM methodology behind what just got described.

Johann's bridge to the camera
"So a lot of what [guest] just described — the ICP work, the signals, the sequencing — looks specific to their business, but it's actually a pattern. Let me walk through the GTM framework we use across every Walego account so you can see how it applies to yours."
Johann's talking points (he speaks; the guest can interject)
T1.ICP strategy

How Walego defines an ICP: not just firmographics, but the signal stack that tells us when a company is ready to be approached. (TAM → TAL → tiered).

T2.Qualification logic

How we separate fit from intent. Why most outbound fails on this. The qualification gates we apply before a contact enters a sequence.

T3.Personalization methodology

Personalization at scale: not "Dear {first_name}". The 3 personalization angles we layer (role, signal, context) and how AI handles each.

T4.Outbound philosophy

Value-first plays vs ask-first plays. Why "book a demo" sequences are dying and what replaces them.

T5.GTM structure

How outbound, ads, content, and signals reinforce each other. Walego is the engine; the system is the moat.

T6.AI qualification approach

Where AI actually moves the needle (signal reading, personalization, qualification) and where it shouldn't be (the human conversation itself).

Johann's close (~30 sec)
"That's the methodology. [Guest] applied it inside [their business] and it generated [hero number]. Same framework works in [other field], in [other field], and we run a different version of it for every Walego account. If anything I just said maps to your business, you know where to find us. Thanks [first name] — really appreciate you doing this."
