PRODUCT 1 1 per day / field TIER · VOLUME + RETENTION

Field-Specific Win Channels

One Slack channel per client field. Clients share their wins. Walego amplifies them daily.

What it is. One Slack channel per client field. Clients post their own wins inside (a quick message or a campaign screenshot). Marketing publishes 1 win per channel, per day, to the website and LinkedIn. Proof for prospects + retention for clients.
How it works

One channel per field

  • One channel per client field on Slack + WhatsApp:
    #wins-consultancy #wins-sales #wins-agency + more later
  • Acquisition: already in our Slack/WhatsApp → direct invite. Not yet in → Brevo email from Johann inviting them to join their field's channel.
  • Two sources of proof: clients post their own wins (text or campaign screenshot) + Marketing pulls screenshots from Walego app analytics
  • Johann welcomes every new client, explains the channel idea, asks for best-campaign screenshots + results
  • Goal: 1 published win per day, per channel
Timeline

Same-day morning ritual

D 0 AMOpen #wins-{field} channels, pick the strongest win of the day · Marketing
D 0 PMDesign the post per field · Marketing
D 0 PMPublish to website + LinkedIn, tagged by field · Marketing
DONE1 win published per field + saved to our library · ✓
Where it goes

High-frequency, field-tagged distribution

1 · Website
Live wins ticker on walego.co Proof blocks on /use-cases/{field} Hover for details · animated reframing
2 · Social media (LinkedIn)
LinkedIn micro-posts, field-tagged
3 · Other channels
Sales decks & cold outreach Brevo newsletter
Ownership

Clients post, marketing publishes

  • Clients: post wins in their field's channel
  • Johann: welcomes clients, explains the channel idea, asks for best-campaign screenshots + results
  • Marketing: runs the channels, collects shared screenshots, publishes 1 win/day per field

Daily proof + client retention. The channels become a community moat.

PRODUCT 2 2 clients / week TIER · HUMAN & EMOTIONAL

Selfie Transformation Videos

Successful clients, alone on camera, telling their story in their own words.

What it is. Each week, 2 existing clients film a 1–2 min selfie video on their phone, answering our questions. Marketing edits each one (subtitles, hooks, branding, thumbnails) into a polished testimonial.
How we reach clients

Brevo email, sent as Johann

  • Audience: existing Walego clients only. Priority: clients already active in P1 #wins channels
  • Friday: Marketing sends Johann next week's brief + nominees list
  • Johann picks 2 clients per week
  • Brevo sends the brief as Johann + a sample selfie video from our team (so clients see what's expected)
  • Auto follow-ups at D+4 and D+8 if no submission

PRODUCT 3 1 per month TIER · AUTHORITY & ELITE

Premium GTM Sessions

Johann + flagship client, online. Strategic conversation, not a testimonial.

What it is. Once a month, Johann sits down online with a flagship client for a 15–30 min strategic conversation. Three parts: client story → Walego partnership → Johann explains the method. Positions Walego as expertise + methodology, not software. Phase 1 launch: June 2026 (preparation in May).
How to reach clients

Personal invitation, flagship only

  • Johann hand-picks 1 flagship client / month (Elite, agencies, strong ROI)
  • Direct personal invite from Johann
  • 100% online on Google Meet (recorded on Riverside)
  • Marketing writes the full script & question set for both sides
