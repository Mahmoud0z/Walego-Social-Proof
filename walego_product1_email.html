Slack channel invitation email

Brevo template, sent from Johann's address to existing Walego clients to invite them into their field-specific #wins-{field} channel. One template, dynamic channel name + link.

Email 1 · Initial invite

Subject (pick one)

Welcome to #wins-{field} — your Walego community channel

{first_name}, see what other Walego {field}s are landing

From

Johann · johann@walego.co · (sent via Brevo)

Body

Hey {first_name},

Johann here.

Quick one. We just opened a Slack channel for Walego clients in your field: #wins-{field}. It's a small community where the people using Walego the way you do ({field}) share what's actually working.

Two things go in there:

  • Quick wins — "booked first meeting in 4 hours!"
  • Campaign screenshots + one line on how Walego helped

That's it. Two-line posts, no pressure. I'm in there too and react to every post.

You get three things back:

  • See what's working for peers in your exact field (real numbers, real campaigns)
  • Get tagged when we feature your wins on walego.co and LinkedIn
  • Direct line to me when you have something to share or ask

Join in 1 click:

Join #wins-{field}

See you in there.

— Johann
Co-founder, Walego · walego.co

Brevo variables: {first_name}, {field} (one of: consultancy / sales / agency / …), {slack_invite_link} for the button URL.

Why this email works

  • Personal sender (Johann's address) lifts open rates vs marketing@
  • Field-specific framing — they see their own segment in the channel name
  • Light ask — two-line posts, not a 30-min interview
  • Triple benefit — peer insight + amplification + founder line
  • One CTA — the join button, nothing else competing for the click
